# Deferred: Enterprise Admin Hierarchy

The first release intentionally supports one organization per student and
three fixed roles. These items are deferred until measured adoption requires
them:

- Multiple organization memberships and organization switching
- Nested administrators, custom permissions, and delegated managers
- Email delivery through SES, approval queues, and moderated publishing
- Cross-organization content sharing and reusable content marketplaces
- Billing, SSO, SCIM provisioning, compliance exports, and retention policies
- Advanced asynchronous analytics pipelines and custom reporting
